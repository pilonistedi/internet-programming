Admissions

Admission Requirements

  • KCSE Mean Grade of C+ and above for Undergraduate programs
  • KCSE Mean Grade of C- and above for Diploma programs
  • Completed application form
  • Copies of academic certificates

Application Process

  1. Fill in the online application form
  2. Submit required academic documents
  3. Pay the application fee
  4. Receive admission confirmation

Tuition Fees

Program Duration Annual Fees
Undergraduate 4 Years Ksh 120,000
Diploma 2-3 Years Ksh 80,000
